# 葉片動畫圖層

使用內建 image_gen，transparent_background=true。PNG 透明圖層保存在 dist/assets/taipei-foliage.png、dist/assets/paris-foliage.png。兩張均為新增前景，與固定建築底圖分開移動。

## taipei

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent foreground animation layer for a Taipei gouache city illustration. A small lush branch entering from the UPPER LEFT corner with a delicate woody twig and loose clusters of oval subtropical green leaves. Delicate handmade gouache and colored-pencil texture, muted sage and warm olive foliage, soft highlights, natural elegant leaf shapes. Only branch and leaves, genuinely transparent background. Branch connected to top left edge, leaves spreading gently toward center and downward; rest of canvas transparent. Not a complete tree, no buildings, no sky, no river, no lettering, no border or shadow backdrop. Wide 3:2 composition.

## paris
